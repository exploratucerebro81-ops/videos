# NOTES FOR BEST RESULTS

## Color Scheme
- **Dark backgrounds:** Charcoal, dark blue, or black
- **Accent colors:** Gold (formula), Green (success), White (text)
- **Avoid:** Bright reds, too many colors (keep it professional)

## Text Guidelines
- **Fonts:** Bold sans-serif (Helvetica, Montserrat, Inter)
- **Size:** Minimum 48px for main text, 32px for secondary
- **Contrast:** White/light text on dark backgrounds for maximum readability

## Animation Speed
- **Entrances:** 0.3-0.5 seconds (fast but not jarring)
- **Holds:** Let key information sit for 2-3 seconds so viewers can read
- **Transitions:** Cross-fade (0.2-0.3 seconds) between scenes

## Sound Design
- Background music: Motivational, uplifting (royalty-free from YouTube Audio Library)
- No sound effects needed, keep it clean
- Let voiceover be the star
