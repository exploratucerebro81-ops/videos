# Google Vids Prompts · Video 01 (10 seconds)
## "Which one are you? (Money Flow)"

**Total duration:** 10 seconds max  
**Language:** English (recommended by Google Vids)  
**Format:** Vertical 9:16 for social  
**Use these prompts in Google Vids to generate each scene**

---

## SCENE 1 (0-1 seconds) · OPENING HOOK

**Voiceover:** "Which one are you?"

### Google Vids Prompt:

```
Generate a 1-second opening title card. 
Text: "WHICH ONE ARE YOU?" in large, bold, white sans-serif font (Helvetica or Montserrat Bold).
Background: Dark solid color (dark gray or black).
Animation: Text appears with a quick pop-in effect, slightly bouncy.
Style: Minimalist, high contrast, attention-grabbing.
Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical.
```

---

## SCENE 2 (1-5 seconds) · THE WRONG WAY (RED/GRAY)

**Voiceover:** "This way: earn, spend, save nothing."

### Google Vids Prompt:

```
Create a 4-second animated flow diagram showing money management.
Left side: Icon of a paycheck or money bag entering (green arrow pointing right).
Middle: Icon of spending (cash, shopping bags) leaving the middle box (red arrow pointing right).
Right side: Icon of a piggy bank, but EMPTY (gray, depleted look).
Labels (small text): "EARN" → "SPEND" → "SAVE (nothing)"
Colors: Use gray and desaturated tones for this "wrong" flow to signal it's not ideal.
Animation: Money flows left to right smoothly, then the savings box appears empty with a sad shake animation.
Text overlay at bottom (small): "❌ 90% of people"
Music: None (just voiceover)
Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical, centered.
```

---

## SCENE 3 (5-9 seconds) · THE RIGHT WAY (GREEN/GOLD)

**Voiceover:** "Or this way: earn, save FIRST, then spend."

### Google Vids Prompt:

```
Create a 4-second animated flow diagram, SAME LAYOUT as Scene 2 but REORDERED.
Left side: Icon of a paycheck or money bag entering (green arrow).
Middle: Icon of piggy bank (FILLED, glowing green or gold).
Right side: Icon of spending (cash, shopping bags) showing available funds.
Labels (small text): "EARN" → "SAVE FIRST" → "SPEND"
Colors: Bright green and gold tones to signal this is the "right" way. The savings box GLOWS and fills up.
Animation: Money flows left to right. The SAVE box fills first (bars rising), glowing. Then the SPEND box lights up with available money.
Text overlay at bottom (small): "✅ Wealthy people"
Visual effect: Add a subtle glow/shine effect around the savings box to emphasize it's the priority.
Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical, centered, SAME STYLE as Scene 2 for easy comparison.
Duration: 4 seconds total.
```

---

## SCENE 4 (9-10 seconds) · CALL TO ACTION

**Voiceover:** "Find out in my bio."

### Google Vids Prompt:

```
Generate a 1-second closing call-to-action screen.
Show the FinanzAcademy logo (if you have it) or brand name in the center.
Text overlay (large): "Click link in bio ✨"
Background: Gradient (dark to slightly lighter) or solid dark color matching the brand.
Animation: Fade in from the previous scene (cross-dissolve), logo appears with a slight scale-up effect.
Arrow: Add a subtle animated arrow pointing upward (↑) to indicate "go to bio/profile".
Duration: 1-2 seconds, then fade to black.
Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical.
```

## HOW TO USE THESE PROMPTS IN GOOGLE VIDS

1. **Open Google Vids** and start a new project
2. **Set duration:** 10 seconds
3. **Set aspect ratio:** 9:16 (vertical for social media)
4. **Copy each prompt above** one by one
5. **Paste into the Google Vids text-to-video input**
6. **Generate scene** and review
7. **Add voiceover:** Use the audio upload or text-to-speech feature
8. **Chain all 4 scenes** in sequence
9. **Export** as MP4 vertical format

## NOTES FOR GOOGLE VIDS

- **Keep prompts SHORT and SPECIFIC** — the AI works better with clear descriptions
- **Mention colors explicitly** (green for good, red/gray for bad)
- **Use directional language** (flow, arrows, progression)
- **Specify timing** in each prompt (e.g., "4-second scene")
- **Request animations** that show contrast (fill vs. empty, bright vs. gray)
- **Test 2-3 times** — AI generation varies; pick the best version
- **Export vertical** (9:16) for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
